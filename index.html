About CropCure

Plant disease detection and cure, in one place.

CropCure gives farmers and gardeners an accurate, fast way to identify what is affecting their plants, and the know-how to bring them back to full health.

  1. Disease detection

    A deep-learning model analyses a photo of the affected leaf and returns an instant diagnosis across 38 healthy and diseased conditions.

    01

  2. Cures & treatments

    Every diagnosis comes with a matched treatment, from organic practices to targeted fungicides and bactericides, to help your plants recover.

    02

  3. Preventive measures

    Prevention is better than cure. Learn the watering, soil-management and crop-rotation practices that keep disease from taking hold.

    03

How it works

Three steps from leaf to treatment.

No sign-up and nothing to install. The whole diagnosis happens on your device.

  1. 01

    Add a leaf photo

    Drop in a clear, well-lit photo of a single leaf, or pick one of the sample leaves to see it in action.

  2. 02

    AI analyses it locally

    The model runs right in your browser with TensorFlow.js. Your photo never leaves your device.

  3. 03

    Get a treatment plan

    See the crop, the condition and how confident the model is, together with a recommended treatment.

Roadmap

What's coming to CropCure.

Features we are building next, to take you from a single diagnosis to a complete crop-care plan.

  1. Built-in AI advisor

    An assistant that stays current with plant-care research and gives real-time treatment advice, so your plants recover faster.

    Planned

  2. 7-day weather forecasts

    Local forecasts to plan irrigation around rainfall and time fertiliser applications to the weather.

  3. Climate-based crop suggestions

    Recommendations for the best crops to plant in your area, based on the season's predicted weather, to maximise yield.

  4. Smart irrigation

  5. In-app treatment store
